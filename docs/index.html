Privacy policy

ScrollIt, Android package cz.teply.scrollit, is published by Matěj Teplý, developer name Majkey. Contact: majkeylab@gmail.com.

The app has no Internet permission, developer account, analytics, advertisements, payments or developer server. It does not upload screen contents, activity history or personal data.

  • Touch mode: Android Accessibility sends repeated swipe gestures after you press Start in the floating controls. The app does not request screen-content retrieval and does not inspect text, passwords or messages. It does not decide which apps or controls to use.
  • AutoScroll mode: optional Shizuku authorization lets the app inject a continuous touch stream at your chosen speed. This grants privileged input access. Use only when you understand Shizuku's setup. See the official Shizuku guide.
  • Floating controls: the overlay permission displays Start, Stop and other controls over the current app. The foreground notification indicates that the controls are active.
  • Storage: the app privately stores speed, motion settings, selected mode and accessibility consent. Android may back up these preferences according to your device backup settings. The developer cannot access those backups.

ScrollIt is a general-purpose scrolling tool and is not directed at children. It does not create profiles or request age information.

Stop access and delete data

Press Stop to end scrolling or Exit to close the controls. Disable ScrollIt in Android Settings → Accessibility to revoke gesture access; revoke overlay permission in Android settings and optional Shizuku authorization in Shizuku.

Preferences remain until you clear them. Use Android Settings → Apps → ScrollIt → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall to delete local settings and consent. Manage device backups separately in Android backup settings. There is no ScrollIt account or developer-held activity history to delete.

For support correspondence or privacy requests, email majkeylab@gmail.com. Your address and message are received by the developer and email provider to handle your request. Do not send private screen content.
